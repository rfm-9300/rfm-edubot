# Government & Law Enforcement Data Request Policy

**Owner:** Rodrigo Martins
**Effective date:** 2026-06-14
**Last reviewed:** 2026-06-14
**Scope:** All requests from public authorities (law enforcement, courts, regulators, or other government bodies) for the personal data or personal information of users processed by this application, including any data received from Meta Platforms ("Platform Data").

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how we handle any request from a public authority for user personal data. Our default posture is to protect user data and disclose only what we are legally required to disclose, only after verifying the request is lawful.

## 2. Legality review (required)

No user personal data is disclosed to any public authority until the request has been reviewed for legal validity. Every request is checked for:

- **Proper legal instrument** — a valid warrant, court order, subpoena, or statutory request, as required by the relevant jurisdiction.
- **Jurisdiction** — confirmation that the requesting authority has lawful jurisdiction over us and over the data requested.
- **Specificity** — the request identifies specific users/data rather than seeking bulk or open-ended access.
- **Authenticity** — verification that the request genuinely originates from the stated authority.

If the legal basis cannot be verified, no data is disclosed and the request is escalated for legal advice.

## 3. Challenging unlawful requests

We reserve the right to **reject, narrow, or formally challenge** any request that we consider unlawful, overbroad, improperly served, or outside the requesting authority's jurisdiction. Where appropriate we will:

- Request that the authority correct or narrow the request;
- Seek legal counsel before responding;
- Decline to comply with, or pursue available legal channels to challenge, requests that lack a valid legal basis.

## 4. Data minimization

When disclosure is legally required, we disclose **only the minimum personal data necessary** to satisfy the specific, validated request. We do not provide:

- Data outside the scope of the request;
- Entire datasets, database exports, or unrelated user records;
- Data for users not named in the request.

## 5. Documentation

We maintain a written record of every request from a public authority in the **Government Data Request Log** (`government-data-request-log.md`). Each entry records:

- Date received and date responded;
- The requesting authority and the individual/actor making the request;
- The legal instrument relied upon and the jurisdiction;
- The scope of data requested;
- Our legal reasoning and the decision (complied / narrowed / rejected / challenged);
- Exactly what data, if any, was disclosed;
- Whether and how the affected user was notified (where legally permitted).

Records are retained for at least the period required by applicable law.

## 6. User notification

Where not prohibited by law or the terms of a valid legal order, we will make reasonable efforts to notify affected users of a request for their data.

## 7. Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually and updated as our processing, processors, or legal obligations change.

Data Deletion

TheBotsLab lets a business answer its WhatsApp and Instagram messages through an automated assistant and send email from its own Gmail or Google Workspace account. This page explains how to have the data we hold deleted.

Disconnect an account (immediate)

A connected business can remove an account at any time:

  1. From the operator dashboard, open the tenant and remove the channel; or
  2. From Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites, and remove this app.

Either action revokes our access and deletes the stored access token for that account. We also receive an automatic notification from Meta when the app is removed and delete the token on our side.

Disconnect a Google (Gmail) account

  1. In the dashboard, open Settings → Channels, choose Manage on the Email (Google) row and press Disconnect. This deletes the account’s tokens and every email we kept from it, and revokes our access with Google.
  2. You can also remove our access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. We stop sending from the account straight away; to also delete the email we kept, disconnect it in the dashboard or email us as below.

Independently of this, we delete the text of every email 90 days after its date.

Request full deletion of your data

To delete the conversation history, emails and any other data we hold about you, email hello@thebotslab.pt with the account, username or email address involved. We will process the request and confirm by reply, typically within 30 days.

If you were redirected here from Instagram with a confirmation code, your deletion request has been received and is being processed; keep that code for reference.

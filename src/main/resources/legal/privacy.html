Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 June 2026

TheBotsLab (“we”, “us”) operates a messaging assistant that lets a business receive and answer customer messages on WhatsApp and Instagram through an automated AI agent. This policy explains what data we process when a business connects an account and when its customers send messages.

1. Who this applies to

Two groups: the business that connects its own WhatsApp or Instagram professional account to our service, and the end users who message that business.

2. Data we collect

  • Connected account data — when a business connects an Instagram account, we receive and store its Instagram account ID, username, and an access token issued by Meta. For WhatsApp we store the phone number ID and access token. Tokens are used only to send and receive messages on that account’s behalf and are never shown back to the browser.
  • Message data — the content, sender ID, and timestamps of direct messages sent to a connected account, plus the replies we generate, so the assistant can maintain conversation context.
  • Operational data — delivery status and minimal logs (identifiers and timestamps, not message bodies) used to run and debug the service.

3. How we use it

Solely to deliver the service: receive incoming messages, generate a relevant reply with an AI model, and send that reply back on the same channel. We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or build cross-site profiles.

4. Third parties we share with

  • Meta Platforms — the WhatsApp Business and Instagram Graph APIs, to receive and send messages.
  • OpenRouter / the underlying LLM provider — message text is sent to generate a reply. We do not send access tokens to the model provider.
  • Our database (MongoDB) — message and account data is stored on infrastructure we control.

5. Retention

We keep connected-account data for as long as the account stays connected, and conversation data for as long as needed to provide the service. When a business disconnects an account — or Meta sends us a deauthorization for it — we remove the stored access token and stop processing messages for that account.

6. Your rights & deleting your data

You can request access to, or deletion of, the data we hold about you at any time. See our Data Deletion page, or email us at hello@thebotslab.pt. A business can also disconnect an account at any time from the operator dashboard or from its Instagram settings, which triggers removal of the stored token for that account.

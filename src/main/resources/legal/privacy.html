Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

TheBotsLab (“we”, “us”) operates a CRM with a messaging assistant: a business receives and answers customer messages on WhatsApp and Instagram through an automated AI agent, keeps its clients, quotes and invoices, runs automations it sets up, and can send email from its own Gmail or Google Workspace account. This policy explains what data we process when a business connects an account and when its customers write to it.

1. Who this applies to

Two groups: the business that uses our service and connects its own WhatsApp, Instagram or Google account to it, and the end users (the business’s customers) who message or email that business.

2. Data we collect

  • Connected account data — when a business connects an Instagram account, we receive and store its Instagram account ID, username, and an access token issued by Meta. For WhatsApp we store the phone number ID and access token. For Google, see section 5. Tokens are used only to act on that account’s behalf and are never shown back to the browser.
  • Message data — the content, sender ID, and timestamps of direct messages sent to a connected account, plus the replies we generate, so the assistant can maintain conversation context.
  • Business records — the clients, quotes, invoices, bookings and payments the business keeps with us, and the automations it sets up: their runs, the approvals and tasks they create, and the messages and emails they send.
  • Operational data — delivery status and minimal logs (identifiers and timestamps, not message bodies) used to run and debug the service.

3. How we use it

Solely to deliver the service to the business: receive incoming messages, generate a relevant reply with an AI model, send that reply back on the same channel, run the automations the business turned on, and send the emails the business or its automations write. We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or build cross-site profiles.

4. Third parties we share with

  • Meta Platforms — the WhatsApp Business and Instagram Graph APIs, to receive and send messages.
  • Google — the Gmail API, to send email from an account the business connected (section 5).
  • OpenRouter / the underlying LLM provider — message text is sent to generate a reply, and the text an automation step works on is sent to produce its result. We do not send access tokens to the model provider.
  • Our database (MongoDB) — message, account and business data is stored on infrastructure we control.

5. Google account and Gmail data

A company administrator can connect the company’s Gmail or Google Workspace account in the dashboard (Settings → Channels → Email (Google)), so quotes, invoices and messages go out from the company’s own address.

What we access

  • Your Google account’s email address (scopes openid and email), to know which account was connected and to show it in the dashboard.
  • Sending email as that account (scope gmail.send). We send only the emails a person in the company sends from the dashboard (for example “Send by email” on a quote), the test email they ask for, and the emails of automations the company set up and turned on. This scope does not let us read your mailbox.
  • Reading the inbox (scopes gmail.readonly and gmail.modify) — only if the company turns on “Use my inbox in automations”, which asks Google for this separately. We then read new messages in the inbox to link them to the company’s clients, show them on the client’s record and start the automations the company chose (for example, logging a supplier’s bill). We don’t read, send or change anything else.

How we store it

  • Google’s access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and used only by our servers to call Google; they are never sent to the browser.
  • For each email we send or receive we keep the sender, recipients, subject, date, attachment names and the text, so the company sees its email history on the client’s record. The text is deleted 90 days after the email’s date; the subject, recipients and attachment names stay as the record of what was sent. We don’t keep attachment contents.
  • When the company disconnects the account, we delete its tokens and all the email we kept from it, and revoke our access with Google (unless another company on our service still uses the same Google account).

Limited Use

TheBotsLab’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Gmail data only to provide the features described above, which the company can see and control in the dashboard.
  • We don’t transfer it to others, except to the AI model provider when an automation the company set up works on an email (for example, drafting a reply), to produce that result only; as needed for security or to comply with the law; or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of our business, with your explicit consent beforehand.
  • We never use Gmail data for advertising, and we never use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person reads Gmail data, unless the company asks us to for support, it is needed for security (such as investigating abuse), or the law requires it.

6. Retention

We keep connected-account data for as long as the account stays connected, and conversation data for as long as needed to provide the service. Email text is deleted after 90 days (section 5). When a business disconnects an account — or Meta sends us a deauthorization for it — we remove the stored access token and stop processing messages for that account; disconnecting a Google account also deletes the email kept from it.

7. Your rights & deleting your data

You can request access to, or deletion of, the data we hold about you at any time. See our Data Deletion page, or email us at hello@thebotslab.pt. A business can also disconnect an account at any time from the dashboard, from its Instagram settings, or from its Google Account (Third-party connections), which ends our access to that account.
